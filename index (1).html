Introducing AquaCharge

One power bank. Two ways to refill it.

AquaCharge supports fast USB‑C charging while also harvesting supplemental energy from flowing water through a compact micro‑hydroelectric module — built for travel, camping, disaster preparedness, field work, and remote environments.
